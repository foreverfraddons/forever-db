WoW Forever database (forever-db / ForeverDB)

This data is merged from several open World of Warcraft Classic / WoW Forever databases,
cross-checked against each other, plus in-game recordings made by WoW Forever players
with Command Center's recorder. The merged data as a whole is distributed under the
GNU General Public License v3.0 (see LICENSE-GPL.txt next to this file).

Sources and their licences:

- QuestieDB (https://github.com/Questie/QuestieDB) - WoW Forever quests, NPCs, items,
  objects and map positions, including its Forever corrections. Offered by the Questie team
  under the GNU GPL v3; their LICENSE.md notes that older Questie contributions were never
  formally licensed. Questie's name and logo are not used.
- VMaNGOS world database (https://github.com/vmangos/core) - GNU GPL v2 or (at your option)
  any later version, used here under GPL v3.
- cMaNGOS classic-db (https://github.com/cmangos/classic-db) - GNU GPL v3.
- AllTheThings (https://github.com/ATTWoWAddon/AllTheThings) - MIT licence, copyright (c) 2026
  AllTheThings WoW Addon; see LICENSE-AllTheThings.txt. Its WoW Forever database sources (quest
  givers, quest levels, positions, rewards, vendors, drops) and the WoW Forever client tables it
  ships (items, flight masters, maps, zones, achievements, criteria, holidays, recipes, item
  effects, transmog sets, battle pets; Blizzard Entertainment's game data, exported by wago.tools).
- LegacyForever (https://github.com/cjber/legacy-forever) - GNU GPL v3 or later: WoW Forever's
  Legacy challenges, exploration areas and reviewed criterion locations.
- Shortest Path Forever (https://github.com/cjber/shortest-path-forever) - GNU GPL v3 or later:
  flight paths and times, boats/zeppelins and their timetables, portals, teleports, dungeon
  entrances, trainers and other town services.
- pfQuest database (https://github.com/shagu/pfQuest) - MIT licence, copyright (c)
  2017-2021 Eric Mauser (Shagu); see LICENSE-pfQuest.txt.
- Drop Tables (https://github.com/valcheffnet/DropTables) - data under CC BY-SA 4.0, credited as
  "Drop Tables contributors, https://github.com/valcheffnet/DropTables"; see LICENSE-DropTables.txt.
  Its loot counts, money, skinning and kill spots recorded on WoW Forever were changed by this
  project (turned into drop chances per kill, gold per kill, skinning chances and map spots, and
  merged with the other sources). They include counts from Blackthornes Farmer (MIT licence,
  copyright (c) 2026 Blackthornes), "Data from Efficient Games (https://efficient.games), licensed
  under CC BY 4.0" and "Data from Everloot (https://everloot.app), CC BY-SA 4.0". CC BY-SA 4.0
  material may be shared under GPL v3 (Creative Commons' compatibility list); the Drop Tables data
  itself stays under CC BY-SA 4.0. The Drop Tables addon's code is not used.
- Zone borders (zbord, abord, agrid) are worked out from the WoW Forever client's own terrain files
  (each ADT map chunk's area ID), read by file ID from wago.tools for the client build. The way the
  terrain is read follows OpenMap's tools (MIT, Copyright (c) 2026 OpenMap contributors,
  https://github.com/openmap-world/openmap); no OpenMap code or data is copied.
- Translations: the WoW Forever client's own texts per language (game data, read from wago.tools by build),
  QuestieDB's Forever translations (l10n/Forever, GPL-3.0) and pfQuest's (MIT).
- WoW Forever in-game recordings (Command Center's recorder).
